# The West corridor: British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan and Manitoba -- 2026-11-22

Copy by the director, waiting on Orinks's approval; only then does
`cargo tools bake-billboards --sheet <this file> --write` put it on the
legs. Both directions of an attraction read the same line.

Part one, below: the thirteen Highway 1 and 97 signs from the Alaska
Highway sheet, re-placed on the legs the West corridor split the old
Surrey to Prince George road into (Surrey, Abbotsford, Chilliwack, Hope,
Cache Creek, Williams Lake, Quesnel, Prince George). Each stands where it
stood before: the old sign's point on the road, projected onto the new
leg (within 0.02 miles), except Barkerville northbound, which moved to ten
miles before Quesnel so the sign comes before the turn for Highway 26
rather than after it, and Hell's Gate southbound, two miles earlier to
clear the Boston Bar museum's callout. Facts and sources are as on the Alaska Highway sheet.

Part two, at the end: the corridor's own places, with the director's
copy and rulings. British Columbia, Saskatchewan and Manitoba license
boards, so a place there gets a billboard about ten miles before it on
each road in. Alberta bans boards, and so does a park, so a place there
gets a plain landmark line at the place itself. Every point is the
OpenStreetMap element named under the place, projected onto the leg.
