# Glossary: Turkish

The game addresses the player as "siz" ("2 saat sürüş süreniz kaldı"). The
register is plain, friendly and spoken: a co-driver talking in the cab, not a
manual. Short sentences, everyday words, no abbreviations a screen reader
would spell out.

Units: distances stay in the unit the game uses, miles and feet or
kilometers and meters. Only the unit word is translated ("mil", "fit",
"kilometre", "metre"); the numbers never change. Key names (Enter, Escape,
Space, Tab, Page Up, F1...) and place names stay in English.

Plurals: a Turkish noun after a number stays singular ("3 mil", "2 saat"),
so most lines need no `{count:...}` choice. Where a choice is needed, the
forms are one, then other.

Placeholders and suffixes: a case suffix on a placeholder has to follow the
sound of a name the game fills in later ("Denver'a", "Chicago'ya"), which a
translation cannot know. Do not attach suffixes to `{city}` or any other
name. Rephrase so the name stands alone: "Varış: {city}." or
"{city} yönünde", "{city} şehrine".
