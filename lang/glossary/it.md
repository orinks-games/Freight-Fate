# Glossary: Italian

The game addresses the player as "tu" ("Hai 2 ore di guida"). The register
is plain, friendly and spoken: a co-driver talking in the cab, not a manual.
Short sentences, everyday words, no abbreviations a screen reader would spell
out.

Units: distances stay in the unit the game uses, miles and feet or
kilometers and meters. Only the unit word is translated (miglia, piedi,
chilometri, metri); the numbers never change. Key names (Enter, Escape,
Space, Tab, Page Up, F1...) and place names stay in English.
