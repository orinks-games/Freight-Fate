# Glossary: Japanese

The game speaks in polite です・ます style and addresses the player
without pronouns where natural; avoid "あなた". The register is plain,
friendly and spoken: a co-driver in the cab talking, not a manual. Use
Japanese punctuation (、。「」), and no space is needed between sentences.

Units: distances stay in the unit the game says, miles and feet or
kilometers and meters; only the unit word is translated (マイル, フィート,
キロメートル, メートル). Numbers never change and stay as Arabic digits.
Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1 and the rest) and place
names stay in English, in Latin letters. A screen reader reads Latin-letter
names and digits inside Japanese text fine, so keep them exactly as they
are. Japanese has no plural forms; write one form.
