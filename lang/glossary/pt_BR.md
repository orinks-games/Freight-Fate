# Glossary: Brazilian Portuguese

The game addresses the player as "você", with verbs in the third person
("Você tem 2 horas de direção"). The register is plain, friendly and spoken:
a co-driver talking in the cab, not a manual. Short sentences, everyday
words, no abbreviations a screen reader would spell out.

Units: distances stay in the unit the game uses, miles and feet or
kilometers and meters. Only the unit word is translated (milhas, pés,
quilômetros, metros); the numbers never change. Key names (Enter, Escape,
Space, Tab, Page Up, F1...) and place names stay in English.
