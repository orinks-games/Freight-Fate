# Glossary: German

The game addresses the player with "du", capitalized only at the start of a
sentence. The register is plain, friendly and spoken: a co-driver talking
from the next seat, not a manual. Short sentences, everyday words.

Distances stay in the units the game uses, miles and feet or kilometers and
meters. Only the unit word is translated ("Meilen", "Fuß", "Kilometer",
"Meter"); the numbers never change. Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab,
Page Up, F1 and the rest) and place names stay in English.
