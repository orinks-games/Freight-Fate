# Glossary: Spanish

The game speaks neutral international Spanish that a player in Latin America
or Spain understands, and addresses the player with "tú". The register is
plain, friendly and spoken: a co-driver talking from the next seat, not a
manual. Short sentences, everyday words.

Distances stay in the units the game uses, miles and feet or kilometers and
meters. Only the unit word is translated ("millas", "pies", "kilómetros",
"metros"); the numbers never change. Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab,
Page Up, F1 and the rest) and place names stay in English.
