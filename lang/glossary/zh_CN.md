# Glossary: Simplified Chinese

The game addresses the player as "你", never "您". The register is plain,
friendly and spoken: a co-driver in the cab talking, not a manual. Use
full-width Chinese punctuation (，。：；？！“”), and no space is needed
between sentences.

Units: distances stay in the unit the game says, miles and feet or
kilometers and meters; only the unit word is translated (英里, 英尺, 公里,
米). Numbers never change and stay as Arabic digits. Key names (Enter,
Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1 and the rest) and place names stay in
English, in Latin letters. A screen reader reads Latin-letter names and
digits inside Chinese text fine, so keep them exactly as they are. Chinese
has no plural forms; write one form.
