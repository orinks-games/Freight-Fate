# Glossary: Polish

The game speaks to the player in the second person singular, with "ty"
forms ("Masz 30 minut", "Zjedź w prawo"), as Polish games usually do. The
register is plain, friendly and spoken: a co-driver in the cab talking, not
a manual. Polish past tense marks gender ("dostarczyłeś", "dostarczyłaś");
the game does not know the player's gender, so prefer present tense or an
impersonal form ("Ładunek dostarczony") over a gendered past tense.

Units: distances stay in the unit the game says, miles and feet or
kilometers and meters; only the unit word is translated (mila, stopa,
kilometr, metr). Numbers never change and stay as Arabic digits. Key names
(Enter, Escape, Space, Tab, Page Up, F1 and the rest) and place names stay
in English, in Latin letters. Plurals: when a word depends on a number,
write the three forms after the placeholder in the order one, few, many,
for example `{count} {count:mila|mile|mil}` (see "Plurals" in
`lang/README.md`).
