# Glossary: French

The game addresses the player with "vous". The register is plain, friendly
and spoken: a co-driver talking from the next seat, not a manual. Short
sentences, everyday words.

Distances stay in the units the game uses, miles and feet or kilometers and
meters. Only the unit word is translated ("miles", "pieds", "kilomètres",
"mètres"); the numbers never change. Key names (Enter, Escape, Space, Tab,
Page Up, F1 and the rest) and place names stay in English.
