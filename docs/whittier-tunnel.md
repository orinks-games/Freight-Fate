# Whittier tunnel

Whittier is a real road endpoint with no freight facilities or generated jobs.
The available terminal and port references do not provide a verified freight
street address and coordinates, so the map does not invent one.

The Anton Anderson Memorial Tunnel is a separate one-lane, 25 mile-per-hour
leg between Bear Valley staging and Whittier. It is 2.5 miles long; the map's
paid-mile convention rounds that path length to 3 miles. The Anchorage to
Bear Valley road leg was checked with public Valhalla truck costing on
2026-10-09: 55.9443 route miles, recorded as 56 paid miles.

The schedule data claims only 2026-01-01 through 2027-04-30. It uses the
published seasonal pattern: winter 7:00 AM–10:45 PM and summer 5:30 AM–11:15
PM, with fifteen-minute directional openings every thirty minutes. Outside
those dates, hours are not published. The gate reads these times on Alaska Time, the tunnel's own clock, on the date the
game's calendar shows. The verified toll direction is Bear
Valley to Whittier, not Whittier to Bear Valley; the $137 Class D ticket is a
round trip and is charged only on that direction.

The normal-game combination convention remains the loaded-semi routing
profile: the game's 73.5-foot WB-67 tractor-semitrailer combination. This is below
the 75-foot tunnel limit. Special Class E openings, permits for 10–11 foot
wide or 14–15 foot high loads, sub-freezing five-minute openings, unscheduled
train delays, rail carriage of placarded hazardous materials, parking, and
freight facilities remain deferred.
