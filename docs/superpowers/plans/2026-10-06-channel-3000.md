# Channel 3000 on 87.7, and LWorks Radio on the dial

Owner's ask, 2026-10-06: put the Channel 3000 programming on the in-game
radio, keep the music pack from getting outrageously big, and add LWorks
Radio to the web dial. The changelog mentions LWorks Radio only. **No
changelog entry, and no user-manual text, mentions Channel 3000**: players
find it.

## What Channel 3000 is

The television station from the owner's sketch shows, heard on the radio at
87.7 FM. An FM radio really can pick up analog TV channel 6's sound there. It
plays its own programmes: daytime shows, night shows, the After Hours
segments, Grimatonics songs and Phoneme's Flying Circus. Between them it
plays its own glue: idents, continuity announcers, commercials and short
sketches.

The station brief is `docs/channel3000/STATION.md`. Its
sections "How the station runs in the game" and "What plays when" are the
spec for the schedule below. `DAYTIME.md` and `NIGHT.md` beside it describe
the shows.

## The audio

The clips stay out of git. They sat on an orphan `channel3000-clips` branch
until 2026-10-11; now the owner keeps them locally, and the published pack
stores each clip unchanged. The text files moved to `docs/channel3000/`.

- `clips/<key>.opus`: every clip, already measured and normalised like
  music.pak (one static gain to -18 LUFS, -1 dBTP ceiling, made with this
  repo's `tools/encode_music_opus.py` and `tools/loudness.py`). Ogg Opus at
  48 kbps stereo, 48 kHz. About 110 MB in all.
- `clips.json`: the manifest (below).
- `stage_clips.py`: how they were made, from the owner's local masters. It is
  provenance only; the cloud can't run it.
- `STATION.md`, `DAYTIME.md`, `NIGHT.md`: the station's briefs.

**Never copy the `.opus` files into a commit.**

## Size: its own pack, not music.pak

About 300 minutes of audio. In `music.pak` at its 80 kbps that would be
178 MB more, on a pack the game already reads whole into memory (382 MB).
Instead:

- **`channel3000.pak`**: the same FFPK format (`tools/assets_pack.py`,
  `crates/ff-core/src/assets_pack.rs`), keys `c3k/<key>.opus`, entries
  stored rather than deflated (Opus doesn't compress). About 110 MB.
- **Opened only when the player first tunes to Channel 3000**, never at
  startup, and never merged into the music pack's memory. `CombinedPack`
  routes `c3k/` names to it. If the pack is missing, Channel 3000 is not on
  the dial (no dead air).
- **Downloaded and SHA-pinned like the music pack.** Follow
  `tools/build_release.py`'s `ensure_music_pack` and
  `DEFAULT_MUSIC_SHA256`, with `https://www.orinks.net/downloads/channel3000.pak`.
  Publishing it to the site is a separate step the owner approves, so build it
  and pin its SHA, but don't upload. `assets/channel3000.pak` is
  gitignored like `music.pak`. `music.pak` is unchanged.
- **A new `tools/build_channel3000.py`** packs a folder of clips (the
  branch's `clips/`) into `assets/channel3000.pak` and writes
  `data/channel3000.json` from `clips.json`. It checks that every manifest
  key has a clip and every clip a manifest row.

## The manifest

`data/channel3000.json` is committed and baked into `world.ffdata`
(`TEXT_FILES` in `crates/ff-core/src/data/baked/mod.rs`) like the radio
catalogs. It is `clips.json` as-is:

`{schema: 1, notes, frequency_mhz: 87.7, dayparts: {day: [5,19], prime: [19,22], late: [22,2], overnight: [2,5]}, clips: [...]}`

Each clip is `{key, kind, dayparts, opener, title, duration_s, ...loudness
report fields}`:

- `kind`: `programme`, `ident`, `continuity`, `ad` or `short`.
- `dayparts`: which of `day`, `prime`, `late`, `overnight` it may play in.
- `opener`: true on exactly one continuity line per daypart, the one that
  opens it.
- `title`: what "now playing" says. Programmes carry the show's name; glue
  says "Channel 3000"; ads say "a commercial break".
- `duration_s`: measured from the encoded file. The schedule runs on it.

As staged, it has 96 clips: 55 programmes, 20 continuity lines, 10 idents,
8 ads and 3 shorts. Two more programmes, `night_starhauler_01` and `_02`
(prime), may be added to the branch later; the tool and the tests must not
pin the counts.

## The schedule (ff-core, headless, in its own module)

Dayparts follow the truck's LOCAL hour, `Trip::local_hour()` in
`crates/ff-core/src/sim/trip/place_time.rs`:

| daypart | hours |
|---|---|
| `day` | 5:00 to 19:00 |
| `prime` | 19:00 to 22:00 |
| `late` | 22:00 to 2:00 |
| `overnight` | 2:00 to 5:00 |

- **A programme is never cut off.** When one ends, the station checks the
  daypart.
- **Between programmes:** an ident for the daypart, then one, sometimes
  two, of: a continuity line, one or two ads, a short.
- **When the daypart has changed** since the last programme began, the glue
  is the ident, then that daypart's opener, and nothing else, and the next
  programme comes from the new daypart.
- **Programmes** come from the daypart's pool in a shuffled order, with no
  repeat until the pool is used up. The same show (the key without its
  `_NN`, for example `day_weigh_in`) never plays twice in a row when another
  show is available. Glue avoids its last few picks the same way.
- **Seeding and continuity:** it is deterministic from a seed, like the other
  stations' rotations (`crates/ff-core/src/radio_rotation.rs`, crc32
  seeds), so a test can pin a sequence. It runs on the same airtime clock as
  the other rotations (`advance_radio_airtime` in
  `crates/freight-fate/src/states/driving_updates/radio.rs`), and the next
  clip is chosen from the local hour at the moment the previous one ends.
  The station keeps going while the radio is on another station or off, and
  tuning in drops you mid-clip, as the other stations do.
- **Now playing** (Shift+Y, `speak_radio_now_playing`): "Now playing on
  Channel 3000: Rise and Grind." using the clip's `title`.

## The station row

A row in `data/radio_catalog.json`:

- id `channel-3000`, name "Channel 3000", empty `call_sign` (a fictional
  call sign must not squat a real FCC one; see `docs/radio-sweep-brief.md`);
- format "television", `frequency_mhz` 87.7;
- always available, local (no stream).

It belongs with the Freight Fate stations (dial group 1), so it is hidden in
Synthesized music mode like them. Update the pinned count of those stations
(`test_ff_music_stations_share_the_ff_dial_group`, 18 to 19) and say why in
